# Rules
1. Use only supplied records and scores; never invent provenance or condition observations.
2. Treat priority scores as planning aids, not independent determinations of cultural significance.
3. Do not recommend irreversible treatment or intervention.
4. Flag missing ownership, permissions, consultation, and documentation as due-diligence gaps.
5. Respect community protocols and do not publish restricted locations or sensitive cultural information.
6. Require qualified conservator and relevant stakeholder review before action.
